Seatan

The SeaTan Seminar, hosted at SFIT, was a remarkable event that provided students with valuable insights into maritime security and the role of the Indian Coast Guard. Commandant Swapnil Swami and Deputy Commandant Ram Prabhu led the seminar, sharing their expertise on coastal surveillance, search and rescue operations, anti-smuggling measures, and disaster response. Their cadets also contributed by offering firsthandexperiences of training and life in the force. The interactive Q&A session allowed attendees to engage directly with the officers, gaining a deeper understanding of the challenges and responsibilities in maritime defense. The event not only educated students about national security but also inspired many to explore careers in this vital field. The SFIT administration and organizing team expressed their gratitude to Commandant Swapnil Swami, Deputy Commandant Ram Prabhu, and theircadets for making theseminar a trulyimpactfulexperience.
